# Declined upkeep proposals

Proposals the owner declined after an `/upkeep` survey, one line each, so the
survey doesn't propose them again before their until-date. An issue or PR the
owner wants kept open gets the `upkeep:keep` label instead of a line here.
The upkeep plugin's `skills/upkeep/SKILL.md` has the key format.
