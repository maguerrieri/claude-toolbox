---
description: Scaffold a new gm campaign (pick a system adapter + saves location) and create the starting character.
---

Use the `gm` skill to start a new campaign. The campaign is the **player's**: its premise, truths, safety lines and character come from the player's answers or from the dice, never from your own genre defaults and never from a bundled example.

**Adapter-driven setup.** Some adapters ship their own campaign-launch files (adapter-contract: "Campaign launch"): `rules/campaign-launch.md`, `rules/truths.md`, `rules/character-creation.md`, `rules/sector-creation.md`, resolving `extends:`. Once the adapter is chosen, check for them. Steps 6–8 and 12 say what each one replaces; whatever an adapter doesn't ship keeps the generic step. Where such a file says "choose or roll", the player chooses, and you roll the named table when they say "surprise me" or ask for inspiration. If the adapter ships `rules/campaign-launch.md`, read it before step 6: it gives the order of the exercises and the setup guidance (e.g. setting assumptions) the steps below follow. Where it lets either of two exercises come first (Ironsworn's character and world), keep the order below.

1. **Ask the player** for the **system adapter** (default `generic` — list `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/`, leaving out `abstract: true` bases) and the **saves location** — a directory in *their* space (e.g. `~/rpg/<name>`), never inside the plugin. Just note it for now: nothing is written there until the player has answered steps 2 and 3.
2. **Ask for the premise, every time, and wait for the answer:** *"What's the setting or premise? Or say 'surprise me' and I'll roll one."* This can share a message with step 1, but it is never skipped: not when the player's first message names only an adapter or a path, and not by filling the gap with a premise of your own. (If `$ARGUMENTS` names a setting, that is the answer; confirm it and go on.) A "surprise me" answers only the question it was given: steps 6–8 still ask for truths, a character and a starting location, unless the player has said to roll everything.
3. **Ask for lines & veils, every time, before you build anything:** what's off-limits entirely (lines), and what should happen off-screen (veils)? Record the answer in the player's words, `none` included. Never fill them in yourself, and never carry them over from an example or another campaign. They bind what the dice build: when a rolled word points at a line (*Death* against "death of children"), read the pair in a direction that stays clear of it, or reroll that table and say so. (If the adapter has its own safety moves, such as Starforged's Set a Flag, these answers are the first flags.)
4. **Settle the premise.** The player's premise stands as given. On **"surprise me"**, seed it from the dice, not from your genre instincts: `roll table ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/<adapter>/oracles/action.md --n 3`, then the same for `theme.md` (resolve `extends:`; if the adapter has no such pair, use the generic adapter's), and show both rolls. `--n` draws without repeats; pair the results in order. The first pair is the situation, the second the pressure on it, the third the protagonist's stake or a twist. Offer the premise, with the genre and tone it suggests, for the player to accept, tweak, or reroll.
5. **Scaffold** the saves dir (create it if it doesn't exist) with the campaign-state files per the state schema (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/gm/references/state-schema.md`, the format reference): `campaign.md`, whose front-matter sets `adapter:`, `persona: house` and `saves:` (the dir's absolute path) and whose body has the `## Premise`, `## Truths` and `## Tone & safety` sections; empty `npcs.md` / `threads.md` / `clocks.md` / `locations.md`; and a `log/` directory. **Don't read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/examples/`:** the schema and the adapter's sheet template are the whole format, and an example read "for format" becomes the content. If one is in context anyway, step 9 says what that means.
6. **Establish the truths** about the world and list them under `campaign.md`'s `## Truths`.
   - **The adapter ships `rules/truths.md`:** go through its categories in order. For each, the player chooses one of its options, rolls it (`roll table ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/<adapter>/oracles/truths/<truth>.md`, then any sub-table the result names), customizes one, or skips the category for now; on "surprise me", roll it. Record each as its summary (or, for an option without one, a line in your own words) plus any sub-choice, and note its quest starter and any "your character" prompt for steps 7 and 12. Record any setting assumptions `rules/campaign-launch.md` lists first.
   - **Otherwise:** 2–4 truths, the player's, or on "surprise me" one per `action` + `theme` pair, rolled fresh as in step 4 (`--n` set to the number of truths).

   Keep them in the adapter's idiom (for `generic`, the *Idiom* section of its `adapter.md`: no signature terms or props borrowed from a published system unless the player asks for that flavor).
7. **Create the starting character** in `characters/<name>.md` from the adapter's sheet template, resolving `extends:` (`ironsworn` and `starforged` each ship their own; a variant without one inherits `ironsworn-core`'s).
   - **The adapter ships `rules/character-creation.md`:** walk its steps in order, with the player making each choice it offers (on "surprise me", rolling the tables it names), and fill in the sheet as you go.
   - **Otherwise:** the player's concept, or on "surprise me" one seeded by a fresh `action` + `theme` pair. Use the player's name for them if they gave one. Otherwise roll the adapter's `oracles/character-name.md` when it has one, or coin a name from the setting's own places, trades and landmarks rather than a stock fantasy name; after a "surprise me", don't ask for one.
8. **Build the starting location**, if the adapter ships `rules/sector-creation.md`: walk its steps the same way, writing what they make where it says (settlements and planets to `locations.md`, a connection to `npcs.md` and the sheet, a trouble to `threads.md`). The player may skip it, as the procedure allows. Without that file, follow the starting-location step of `rules/campaign-launch.md` if it has one (Ironsworn's "where your story begins"), or else skip this step.
9. **Check it borrows nothing.** If a bundled example is in your context (say, the player demoed it), reuse none of its names or sentences: rename or rewrite anything you wrote that echoes it. Resemblance the player asked for is fine, and the player's own words are never rewritten.
10. **Version the saves:** run `campaign init <saves-dir> --author "<persona author>" --email "<persona email>"` (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/campaign`) to start a dedicated git repo for the campaign — it defers automatically if the dir is already inside a repo (e.g. an Obsidian vault). Resolve author/email from the `chronicle_identity` of the campaign's persona (`house`, unless the player asked for another).
11. **Turn on autosave:** `campaign bind <saves-dir>`, so from here on every turn's dialogue is logged to `log/raw/` and checkpointed by the plugin's Stop hook.
12. Offer to begin play with `/gm:play`. If the adapter's `rules/campaign-launch.md` has a "begin your adventure" exercise, offer to open the first scene with it: the inciting incident, the scene, and the vow that sets the quest in motion.

$ARGUMENTS may name the setting or the saves path.
