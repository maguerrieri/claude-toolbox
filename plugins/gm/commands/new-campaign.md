---
description: Scaffold a new gm campaign (pick a system adapter + saves location) and create the starting character.
---

Use the `gm` skill to start a new campaign. The campaign is the **player's**: its premise, truths, safety lines and character come from the player's answers or from the dice, never from your own genre defaults and never from a bundled example.

1. **Ask the player** for the **system adapter** (default `generic` — list `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/` for options) and the **saves location** — a directory in *their* space (e.g. `~/rpg/<name>`), never inside the plugin.
2. **Ask for the premise, every time, and wait for the answer:** *"What's the setting or premise? Or say 'surprise me' and I'll roll one."* This can share a message with step 1, but it is never skipped: not when the player's first message names only an adapter or a path, and not by filling the gap with a premise of your own. (If `$ARGUMENTS` names a setting, that is the answer; confirm it and go on.)
   - **"Surprise me"** → seed the premise from the dice, not from your genre instincts. Roll the adapter's inspiration pair three times, `roll table ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/<adapter>/oracles/action.md` then `…/theme.md` (resolve `extends:`; if the adapter has no such pair, use the generic adapter's), and show every roll. Build the premise from the three pairs: the first is the situation, the second the pressure on it, the third the protagonist's stake or a twist. Offer it for the player to accept, tweak, or reroll. For a wider pool, scaffold `campaign.md`'s front-matter first and forge one instead: `/gm:forge hook`, then `roll table <saves-dir>/tables/hook.md`.
   - **Genre and tone** come with the premise: the player's, or the ones a seeded premise suggests, confirmed with the player.
3. **Scaffold** the campaign-state files per the state schema (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/gm/references/state-schema.md`, the format reference): `campaign.md` (with `adapter:` set and `persona: house`), empty `npcs.md` / `threads.md` / `clocks.md` / `locations.md`, and a `log/` directory. **Don't read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/examples/`:** the schema and the adapter's sheet template are the whole format, and an example read "for format" becomes the content. If one is in context anyway (the player demoed it), reuse none of it: no name, premise, truth, tone line or safety line.
4. **Establish 2–4 truths** about the world: the player's, or on "surprise me" one per `action` + `theme` roll. Keep them in the adapter's idiom (for `generic`, its *Idiom* rule: no signature terms or props borrowed from a published system unless the player asks for that flavor).
5. **Ask for lines & veils, every time:** what's off-limits entirely (lines), and what should happen off-screen (veils)? Record the player's answer in `campaign.md`'s tone & safety section, in their words, `none` included. Never fill them in yourself, and never carry them over from an example or another campaign.
6. **Create the starting character** in `characters/<name>.md` from the adapter's `sheet-template.md`: the player's concept, or on "surprise me" one seeded by an `action` + `theme` roll. For the name, roll the adapter's `oracles/character-name.md` when it has one; otherwise ask the player, or build one from the premise's own words.
7. **Check it borrows nothing:** `campaign example-overlap <saves-dir>`. It exits 1 and lists any proper name or truth the new campaign shares with a bundled example, without printing the example. Rename or rewrite each one you invented and re-run until it passes. A name the player chose themself stays; tell them it matches the demo campaign.
8. **Version the saves:** run `campaign init <saves-dir> --author "<persona author>" --email "<persona email>"` (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/campaign`) to start a dedicated git repo for the campaign — it defers automatically if the dir is already inside a repo (e.g. an Obsidian vault). Resolve author/email from the chosen persona's `chronicle_identity`.
9. **Turn on autosave:** `campaign bind <saves-dir>`, so from here on every turn's dialogue is logged to `log/raw/` and checkpointed by the plugin's Stop hook.
10. Offer to begin play with `/gm:play`.

$ARGUMENTS may name the setting or the saves path.
