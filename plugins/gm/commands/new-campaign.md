---
description: Scaffold a new gm campaign (pick a system adapter + saves location) and create the starting character.
---

Use the `gm` skill to start a new campaign. The campaign is the **player's**: its premise, truths, safety lines and character come from the player's answers or from the dice, never from your own genre defaults and never from a bundled example.

1. **Ask the player** for the **system adapter** (default `generic` — list `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/`, leaving out `abstract: true` bases) and the **saves location** — a directory in *their* space (e.g. `~/rpg/<name>`), never inside the plugin. Just note it for now: nothing is written there until the player has answered steps 2 and 3.
2. **Ask for the premise, every time, and wait for the answer:** *"What's the setting or premise? Or say 'surprise me' and I'll roll one."* This can share a message with step 1, but it is never skipped: not when the player's first message names only an adapter or a path, and not by filling the gap with a premise of your own. (If `$ARGUMENTS` names a setting, that is the answer; confirm it and go on.)
3. **Ask for lines & veils, every time, before you build anything:** what's off-limits entirely (lines), and what should happen off-screen (veils)? Asking first lets a seeded premise steer clear of them. Record the answer in the player's words, `none` included. Never fill them in yourself, and never carry them over from an example or another campaign.
4. **Settle the premise.** The player's premise stands as given. On **"surprise me"**, seed it from the dice, not from your genre instincts: roll the adapter's inspiration pair three times, alternating `roll table ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/<adapter>/oracles/action.md` and `…/theme.md` (resolve `extends:`; if the adapter has no such pair, use the generic adapter's), and show every roll. Build the premise from the three pairs: the first is the situation, the second the pressure on it, the third the protagonist's stake or a twist. Offer it, with the genre and tone it suggests, for the player to accept, tweak, or reroll. For a wider pool, scaffold `campaign.md`'s front-matter first and forge one instead: `/gm:forge hook`, then `roll table <saves-dir>/tables/hook.md`.
5. **Scaffold** the saves dir (create it if it doesn't exist) with the campaign-state files per the state schema (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/gm/references/state-schema.md`, the format reference): `campaign.md` (with `adapter:` set, `persona: house`, and its premise and tone & safety sections), empty `npcs.md` / `threads.md` / `clocks.md` / `locations.md`, and a `log/` directory. **Don't read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/examples/`:** the schema and the adapter's sheet template are the whole format, and an example read "for format" becomes the content. If one is in context anyway (the player demoed it), reuse none of it: no name, premise, truth, tone line or safety line.
6. **Establish 2–4 truths** about the world: ask for the player's, or on "surprise me" roll a fresh `action` + `theme` pair per truth. Keep them in the adapter's idiom (for `generic`, the *Idiom* section of its `adapter.md`: no signature terms or props borrowed from a published system unless the player asks for that flavor).
7. **Create the starting character** in `characters/<name>.md` from the adapter's `sheet-template.md`: the player's concept, or on "surprise me" one seeded by a fresh `action` + `theme` pair. Name them the player's way if they gave a name. Otherwise roll the adapter's `oracles/character-name.md` when it has one, or coin a name from the setting's own places, trades and landmarks rather than a stock fantasy name; don't ask again after a "surprise me".
8. **Check it borrows nothing:** `campaign example-overlap <saves-dir>`. It exits 1 on any proper name a bundled example declares, or any sentence that closely copies an example's prose (premise, truths, tone, sheets — not lines & veils, which are the player's), and it quotes only the new campaign's side. Rewrite what you invented and re-run until it exits 0. A name the player chose, or one the dice rolled from a name oracle, stays: re-run with `--allow <name>` and tell the player it matches the demo campaign.
9. **Version the saves:** run `campaign init <saves-dir> --author "<persona author>" --email "<persona email>"` (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/campaign`) to start a dedicated git repo for the campaign — it defers automatically if the dir is already inside a repo (e.g. an Obsidian vault). Resolve author/email from the chosen persona's `chronicle_identity`.
10. **Turn on autosave:** `campaign bind <saves-dir>`, so from here on every turn's dialogue is logged to `log/raw/` and checkpointed by the plugin's Stop hook.
11. Offer to begin play with `/gm:play`.

$ARGUMENTS may name the setting or the saves path.
