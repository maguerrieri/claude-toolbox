---
description: Scaffold a new gm campaign (pick a system adapter + saves location) and create the starting character.
---

Use the `gm` skill to start a new campaign. The campaign is the **player's**: its premise, truths, safety lines and character come from the player's answers or from the dice, never from your own genre defaults and never from a bundled example.

1. **Ask the player** for the **system adapter** (default `generic` — list `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/`, leaving out `abstract: true` bases) and the **saves location** — a directory in *their* space (e.g. `~/rpg/<name>`), never inside the plugin. Just note it for now: nothing is written there until the player has answered steps 2 and 3.
2. **Ask for the premise, every time, and wait for the answer:** *"What's the setting or premise? Or say 'surprise me' and I'll roll one."* This can share a message with step 1, but it is never skipped: not when the player's first message names only an adapter or a path, and not by filling the gap with a premise of your own. (If `$ARGUMENTS` names a setting, that is the answer; confirm it and go on.) A "surprise me" answers only the question it was given: steps 6 and 7 still ask for truths and a character, unless the player has said to roll everything.
3. **Ask for lines & veils, every time, before you build anything:** what's off-limits entirely (lines), and what should happen off-screen (veils)? Record the answer in the player's words, `none` included. Never fill them in yourself, and never carry them over from an example or another campaign. They bind what the dice build: when a rolled word points at a line (*Death* against "death of children"), read the pair in a direction that stays clear of it, or reroll that table and say so.
4. **Settle the premise.** The player's premise stands as given. On **"surprise me"**, seed it from the dice, not from your genre instincts: `roll table ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/adapters/<adapter>/oracles/action.md --n 3`, then the same for `theme.md` (resolve `extends:`; if the adapter has no such pair, use the generic adapter's), and show both rolls. `--n` draws without repeats; pair the results in order. The first pair is the situation, the second the pressure on it, the third the protagonist's stake or a twist. Offer the premise, with the genre and tone it suggests, for the player to accept, tweak, or reroll.
5. **Scaffold** the saves dir (create it if it doesn't exist) with the campaign-state files per the state schema (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/gm/references/state-schema.md`, the format reference): `campaign.md`, whose front-matter sets `adapter:`, `persona: house` and `saves:` (the dir's absolute path) and whose body has the `## Premise`, `## Truths` and `## Tone & safety` sections; empty `npcs.md` / `threads.md` / `clocks.md` / `locations.md`; and a `log/` directory. **Don't read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/examples/`:** the schema and the adapter's sheet template are the whole format, and an example read "for format" becomes the content. If one is in context anyway, step 8 says what that means.
6. **Establish 2–4 truths** about the world and list them under `campaign.md`'s `## Truths`: the player's, or on "surprise me" one per `action` + `theme` pair, rolled fresh as in step 4 (`--n` set to the number of truths). Keep them in the adapter's idiom (for `generic`, the *Idiom* section of its `adapter.md`: no signature terms or props borrowed from a published system unless the player asks for that flavor).
7. **Create the starting character** in `characters/<name>.md` from the adapter's sheet template, resolving `extends:` (`ironsworn` and `starforged` inherit `ironsworn-core`'s `sheet-template.md`): the player's concept, or on "surprise me" one seeded by a fresh `action` + `theme` pair. Use the player's name for them if they gave one. Otherwise roll the adapter's `oracles/character-name.md` when it has one, or coin a name from the setting's own places, trades and landmarks rather than a stock fantasy name; after a "surprise me", don't ask for one.
8. **Check it borrows nothing.** If a bundled example is in your context (say, the player demoed it), reuse none of its names or sentences: rename or rewrite anything you wrote that echoes it. Resemblance the player asked for is fine, and the player's own words are never rewritten.
9. **Version the saves:** run `campaign init <saves-dir> --author "<persona author>" --email "<persona email>"` (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/bin/campaign`) to start a dedicated git repo for the campaign — it defers automatically if the dir is already inside a repo (e.g. an Obsidian vault). Resolve author/email from the `chronicle_identity` of the campaign's persona (`house`, unless the player asked for another).
10. **Turn on autosave:** `campaign bind <saves-dir>`, so from here on every turn's dialogue is logged to `log/raw/` and checkpointed by the plugin's Stop hook.
11. Offer to begin play with `/gm:play`.

$ARGUMENTS may name the setting or the saves path.
