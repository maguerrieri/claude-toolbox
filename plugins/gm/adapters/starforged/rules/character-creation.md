# Create Your Character

*Adapted from* Ironsworn: Starforged Rulebook, *chapter 2, "Create Your Character" (pp. 100–112), © 2022 Shawn Tomkin, licensed [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Abridged for solo play with this adapter: the book's page references now point at this adapter's tables, and its group-play notes are left out. This file is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see `SOURCE.md`).*

Work through the steps in order (the book allows about 20–30 minutes). The character sheet is `sheet-template.md`; the asset cards are in `rules/assets/` (index: `rules/assets.md`). Where a step says to choose or roll, the player chooses; roll the named table when they say "surprise me" or want inspiration.

## 1. Ready the asset cards (p. 104)

Assets come in six types: command vehicle, module, support vehicle, path, companion, and deed. "Then, set aside the deed assets. Those aren't available for a starting character, and aren't needed for this exercise."

## 2. Choose two paths (p. 105)

"Choose any two path assets. Consider how each choice plays into an emerging concept for your character." Be mindful of the established truths: "if there is no magic or supernatural abilities, assets such as KINETIC, FIREBRAND, and SEER might be reimagined as a technological resource, or ignored altogether."

*Need inspiration?* "Roll or pick from the table below and take the two paths associated with your selected background": `oracles/campaign-launch/background-assets.md`.

## 3. Create your backstory (p. 106)

"As you begin play in Starforged, your character is a person with few ties to others. The Forge is a vast galaxy, and your former home—if you had one—is lost to you, forsaken by you, or a distant memory." Consider what happened to separate you from your home and relationships. "Keep it simple. There's no need to invent an elaborate history."

For inspiration, roll or pick on `oracles/campaign-launch/backstory-prompts.md` and elaborate on it, or leave it vague and flesh it out in play.

## 4. Write your background vow (p. 107)

Envision a background vow "to represent a primary motivation or ultimate goal." It was sworn months or years ago, so you don't make the Swear an Iron Vow move for it.

- **Rank: epic.** "Mechanically, it's an epic vow, so every opportunity to Reach a Milestone is just one tick on the vow's progress track."
- Write it in your character's words, such as "I vow to destroy the Iron Talon raider clan."
- Stuck? Roll `oracles/action.md` + `oracles/theme.md`, or `oracles/character/goal.md`, or look back at your truths' quest starters (`rules/truths.md`). Or leave it blank and add it once something occurs to you in play.

## 5. Board your starship (p. 108)

Take the STARSHIP command vehicle asset (`rules/assets/command-vehicle.md`). "The default marked ability provides the basic functions of the ship. You can claim the other two abilities when you Advance. For now, leave them unmarked." Playing solo, "your starship is fully capable of flying and fighting while operated by a single pilot."

- **History:** envision how you obtained or earned the ship, or roll or pick on `oracles/campaign-launch/starship-history.md`.
- **Look and quirks:** what makes it uniquely yours? Envision it, or "roll once or twice" on `oracles/campaign-launch/starship-quirks.md`.
- **Name:** write it on the asset. Stuck? Roll `oracles/starship/starship-name.md`, or save it for later.

## 6. Choose your final asset (p. 110)

Pick one more asset from any of these categories: **module**, **support vehicle**, **companion**, or **path**. "This asset, plus your two paths from earlier in this exercise, give your character a total of three starting assets along with your STARSHIP."

## 7. Set your stats (p. 110)

"Arrange these values across your stats in any order: 3, 2, 2, 1, 1" (edge, heart, iron, shadow, wits). Look at your assets for a stat they lean on: "If you are an INFILTRATOR, you would be well-served with a high value in shadow. If you are a DIPLOMAT, you may rely on your heart. If you are a GEARHEAD, you use your wits to craft and repair."

## 8. Set your condition meters (p. 111)

- Health, spirit, and supply: **5**.
- Momentum **+2**, max momentum **+10**, momentum reset **+2**. (From the sheet, p. 101: max momentum starts at +10 and drops by 1 for each impact; the reset is +2 with no impacts, +1 with one, and 0 with two or more.)
- Set each companion's health and each vehicle's integrity to its max, as its card shows; for your STARSHIP that's 5.

## 9. Envision your character (p. 111)

Note one or two facts about their look, how they act, and what they wear; the rest you discover in play. For example: *Look:* covered in tattoos; *Act:* prefers machines to people; *Wear:* safety goggles and dirty coveralls.

## 10. Name your character (p. 111)

Note a name and pronouns. Optionally, a callsign: "Spacers are typically known more by a callsign than their actual name," and call given and family names "dirtside names." No callsign earned yet? Leave it blank.

For inspiration, `oracles/character/name.md` reads a given name, family name and callsign off one roll; its columns roll on their own as `oracles/character/name/given-name.md`, `oracles/character/name/family-name.md` and `oracles/character/name/callsign.md`.

## 11. Gear up (p. 112)

There's no detailed equipment list: "Your supply meter is an abstract representation of your general readiness." Assume a **spacer kit**: sealed environment suit, flashlights / headlamps, toolkit, medkit, personal communicator. Note any important or unusual personal items ("around four or five items at most"), and the object you swear iron vows upon: what is its history, and why does it matter to you?
