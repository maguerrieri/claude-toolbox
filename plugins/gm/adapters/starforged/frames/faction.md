# faction frame

A power of the forge sector. Its kind is one of Starforged's three faction types (`oracles/faction/type.md`).

## Axes

| Axis | Values |
|------|--------|
| kind | dominion, guild, fringe group |
| goal | expand, profit, convert, survive, hoard |
| method | fleet, trade, doctrine, infiltration, force |
| weakness | overreach, schism, debt, dogma |

## Shape

A sector power with a goal, a method, and a weakness.
