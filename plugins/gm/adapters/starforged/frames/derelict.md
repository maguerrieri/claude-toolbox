# derelict frame

A derelict to explore — a hazard and a find. In Starforged a derelict is a starship or a settlement (`oracles/derelict/`).

## Axes

| Axis | Values |
|------|--------|
| location | planetside, orbital, deep space |
| cause | war, plague, mutiny, the dark, unknown |
| hazard | breach, power-loss, a thing aboard, radiation |
| find | cargo, data, a survivor, tech |

## Shape

A derelict starship or settlement with a cause of ruin, a hazard still aboard, and something worth the risk to find.

## Tagging

[location × cause × hazard]
