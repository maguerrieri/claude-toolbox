# npc frame

A person of the forge frontier with a want and an edge. Roles are drawn from `oracles/character/role.md`; factions are Starforged's three types, or none.

## Axes

| Axis | Values |
|------|--------|
| role | pilot, technician, merchant, mercenary, fugitive |
| faction | dominion, guild, fringe group, unaffiliated |
| want | profit, redemption, belief, data, a person |
| edge | a ship, a secret, a debt owed them, a weapon |

## Shape

A person of the forge frontier with a want.

## Tagging

[role × faction × want]
