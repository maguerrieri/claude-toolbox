# Create Your Character

*Adapted from the* Sundered Isles Guidebook, *"Your Character" (pp. 10–15), "Create Your Character" (pp. 90–95) and "Take Command" (pp. 96–99), © 2024 Shawn Tomkin, licensed [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Abridged and paraphrased for solo play with this adapter: the book's page references now point at this adapter's tables, and its group-play notes are left out. This file is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (see `SOURCE.md`).*

Work through the steps in order, or reorder them as the player likes (the book allows 20–30 minutes for steps 1–6 and 10–20 for step 7). The character sheet is `sheet-template.md`; the asset cards are indexed in `rules/assets.md`. Where a step says to choose or roll, the player chooses; roll the named table when they say "surprise me" or want inspiration. "Once or twice" means the player picks how many.

## Ready the asset deck (pp. 11, 14–15)

A Sundered Isles character draws on a curated deck: every Sundered Isles card (`rules/assets/sundered-isles/`) plus these Starforged cards (`rules/assets/path.md`, `rules/assets/deed.md`), unchanged:

- **Starforged paths:** Armored, Augmented, Bannersworn, Blademaster, Bounty Hunter, Brawler, Courier, Demolitionist, Devotant, Diplomat, Empath, Fated, Firebrand, Fugitive, Gearhead, Haunted, Healer, Kinetic, Leader, Loyalist, Mercenary, Navigator, Outcast, Scavenger, Scoundrel, Seer, Shade, Slayer, Sleuth, Vestige, Veteran.
- **Starforged deeds:** Bonded, Homesteader, Oathbreaker, Revenant, Survivor, Vanguard.

Fit the deck to the truths. These rely on **supernatural or mythic powers**: Devotant, Empath, Firebrand, Haunted, Jinx, Kinetic, Necromancer, Seer, Shade, Shapechanger, Sorcerer, Windbinder; Albatross, Chattering Skull, Dragon, The Kraken; Damned, Undead; Lucky Figurehead, Trophy Vault. These rely on **remarkable technologies**: Armored, Augmented, Construct, Demolitionist, Gearhead; Diving Bell, Flying Machine; Harpoon Cannon, Ironclad Hull, Submersible Mode. In a grounded, historical-feeling setting, leave them out.

The deck is a recommendation: another Starforged card may suit the character, but watch for overlap, since some Sundered Isles cards are reworked Starforged ones (PEDDLER stands in for TRADER). The book also suggests an optional **rule of three** (p. 15): use abilities from no more than three assets on one action, chosen before rolling, and take no more than +3 in adds from assets on a roll.

## 1. Forge your character concept (p. 90)

Sketch the broad strokes, with the three pillars of a Sundered Isles character in mind (p. 10):

- **You are Ironsworn.** What incident, need or responsibility sets you on your quests?
- **You are a seafarer.** What calls you to the sea, and what skills or abilities set you apart?
- **You are a rebel.** What faction, circumstance or obligation do you resist, and who stands with you?

No concept yet? Step 2 generates one; or start with the paths (step 3).

## 2. Seek inspiration (pp. 92–93)

- **Backstory:** what put you on a path of adventure? Roll or choose once or twice on `oracles/getting-underway/create-your-character/your-backstory.md`. It has a cursed version for the cursed die (`adapter.md`); in a setting that leans into the uncanny, roll on `oracles/getting-underway/create-your-character/your-backstory-cursed.md` directly.
- **Characteristics**, each optional; ignore any result that doesn't fit:
  - Obvious at first glance: `oracles/character/first-look.md`, once or twice.
  - Profession, former or current: `oracles/character/role.md` once, then the matching table in `oracles/character/role-details/`.
  - A defining accessory or piece of gear: `oracles/character/trademark-accessories.md`, once or twice.
  - A favored weapon, if armed: `oracles/character/trademark-weapons.md` (a cursed result adds `oracles/character/weapon-detail-cursed.md`).
  - Less obvious or hidden aspects: `oracles/character/revealed-aspect.md` (Character Details), once or twice.
  - What drives you: `oracles/character/goal.md` (Character Goals), once or twice.
  - An object of personal significance: `oracles/treasure/tokens.md`.

## 3. Choose two paths (pp. 90, 94–95)

Pick two path assets from the deck that support an interesting concept, fit the truths, and promise the stories the player wants. For inspiration, `oracles/getting-underway/create-your-character/your-persona.md` pairs a persona with two recommended paths (pick from it, or roll and let the Fates decide). Skip the personas it marks as only for co-op or guided play with allies (Bulwark, Piper, Skipper), which don't suit solo play. Mark each path's starting ability.

## 4. Name your character (p. 91)

Note a name and pronouns, plus a moniker or title if the character is known by one. For inspiration: `oracles/character/name/given-name.md` and `oracles/character/name/family-name.md`, whose rows pair up (take a pair, mix and match, swap them, or use either alone); a moniker from `oracles/character/name/callsign.md` (the Sundered Isles Moniker table, at Starforged's Callsign path); for a dreadful, eldritch, ill-fated or mechanical figure, a cursed moniker starting from `oracles/character/name/moniker-cursed/category.md`.

## 5. Write your background vow (p. 91)

What ambition, need or commitment drives you? Write it as a vow, ranked **epic**; it was sworn long ago, so don't make the Swear an Iron Vow move for it. Unsure? Leave it for later, or look to `oracles/character/goal.md`.

## 6. Set your stats and meters (p. 91)

- **Stats:** arrange 3, 2, 2, 1, 1 across edge, heart, iron, shadow and wits, leaning on what the paths use.
- **Meters:** health, spirit and supply 5 (supply here is your *equipped* supply; a ship's hold is tracked separately, `adapter.md`). Momentum +2, max momentum +10, momentum reset +2. (As in Starforged, each marked impact lowers max momentum by 1, and the reset is +2 with no impacts, +1 with one, and 0 with two or more.)

## 7. Take command (pp. 96–99)

Decide whether the character starts with a ship to command, and what that ship and crew are like.

**Choose your command:**

- **Novice command** (the default start): a minor vessel of little repute, with an unremarkable crew. Note a SAILING SHIP on the sheet. It's an incidental vehicle, not a character asset: no abilities, no modules, and it can't be marked battered or cursed. Make it heavy (integrity 5), medium (4) or light (3); if unsure, light, a small sloop, ketch or cutter. As your primary ship it has up to 5 hold (pp. 17, 96).
- **Worthy command:** an admirable vessel and an able crew. Take the FLAGSHIP command vehicle asset (`rules/assets/sundered-isles/command-vehicle.md`), normally earned with experience, at the start.
- **No command:** no ship of your own, yet. Serve aboard someone else's ship, be stranded or marooned, or keep your adventures ashore for now.

**Ship history.** With a command, how did you claim or earn it? Roll or choose on `oracles/getting-underway/take-command/ship-history.md` (cursed version: `oracles/getting-underway/take-command/ship-history-cursed.md`). Serving under an NPC captain, use it for their ship.

**Ship characteristics**, each optional:

- Size: `oracles/ship/size.md` once; ignore or reroll the fleet results (Flotilla, Fleet, Armada).
- What shows when first sighted: `oracles/ship/first-look.md`, once or twice.
- The symbol on its flag or as a motif: `oracles/ship/symbol.md`.
- Less obvious or hidden aspects of ship or crew: `oracles/ship/details.md`, once or twice.
- Cargo now aboard: `oracles/ship/cargo.md`, up to three times.
- Name: `oracles/ship/name.md`, or name it yourself.

**Crew.** Envision the crew as a whole with `oracles/getting-underway/take-command/crew.md`; a result can feed the backstory and quests, or set the crew at odds with the character. A cursed result adds an eerie aspect from `oracles/getting-underway/take-command/crew-cursed.md`. Individual crewmembers get names and details later, as they matter.

**Final asset.** Choose one more asset from **support vehicle**, **module** (only with a FLAGSHIP), **path** or **companion**, never a deed. To lead the crew, take CREW COMMANDER; to captain pirates, PIRATE CAPTAIN. Or bank the asset and claim it later in play, at no experience cost, once the character is clearer.

**Remaining meters.** Set the integrity of a SAILING SHIP, FLAGSHIP or support vehicle to its max, the ship's hold supply to its max, and a companion's health to its max.
