## The Datasworn edition

The Sundered Isles data comes from the machine-readable edition published by **Datasworn** (https://github.com/rsek/datasworn), at a pinned ref:

- repo `rsek/datasworn`, tag `v0.1.0-prerelease`, commit `e643a75263c1cb3e845a0c0789879ff4e8b49532`
- file `datasworn/sundered_isles/sundered_isles.json`, sha256 `d3c8b8318dc3515eb738d47e65465916b2444d81854e070ad30dc343ce68bfc4`

At that ref, Datasworn tags 327 of the file's objects CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (the oracles, assets and truths) and 12 CC BY 4.0 (the moves and their categories). `plugins/gm/tools/datasworn-import` converts it, together with the starforged file the adapter extends, into this adapter's formats; every file it writes starts with a "Generated by tools/datasworn-import" comment. Changes made: the JSON is converted to Markdown tables and rules files; Datasworn's cross-references are rewritten as file paths; a replacing table is placed at the path of the Starforged table it replaces; the cursed-die links between tables are written out as notes; the indexes and the move list merge the Sundered Isles content with the Starforged content it doesn't replace. One correction is made to the data: the Highlands landmark table (`oracles/overland/region-landmarks/highlands.md`) stops at 50 in Datasworn, and its rows 51–100 are restored from the Guidebook (p. 152). Nothing else is added to or omitted from the moves, assets, tables or truths. To regenerate, or to check the tree against the pin, run `plugins/gm/tools/datasworn-import` (or `--check`).

## The Sundered Isles Guidebook (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)

These files are hand-written for this plugin from the **Sundered Isles Guidebook**, © 2024 Shawn Tomkin, whose text is licensed **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (its copyright page says so). They are abridged and paraphrased for solo play, with the book's page references replaced by this adapter's file paths, and are licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:

- `rules/campaign-launch.md` — from "Getting Underway" (pp. 66–111)
- `rules/character-creation.md` — from "Your Character", "Create Your Character" and "Take Command" (pp. 12–17, 92–101)
- `rules/sector-creation.md` — from "Chart Your Course" and "Faction Influence Grid" (pp. 52, 102–109)
- `adapter.md` — summarizes rules from the Guidebook (the cursed die, regions, supply at sea, mindful worldbuilding), with page references

Page references are to the digital edition released to the Kickstarter backers in April 2024 (the "0426" PDF). A later printing may number some pages differently.
