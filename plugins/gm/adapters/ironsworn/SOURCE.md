## Changes made

To the generated files: the JSON is converted to Markdown tables and rules files; Datasworn's cross-references are rewritten as this adapter's file paths (a move, region or NPC becomes its name); the world truths keep Datasworn's 1d100 ranges, so they can be rolled (the book lists their options without rolls). One upstream data error is corrected: Datasworn gives the second Mysticism truth the third's range (68–100), which the tool sets to 34–67, as every other truth is split (`ERRATA` in the tool). Otherwise nothing is added to or omitted from the moves, assets, tables, truths, regions or NPCs. To regenerate, or to check the tree against the pin, run `plugins/gm/tools/datasworn-import --ruleset classic` (or `--check`); `--help` says how to move the pin.

## Original content

`adapter.md` and `frames/` are written for this plugin. They describe and point at the content above. `adapter.md` paraphrases three points of the rulebook the SRD data doesn't carry, with its page numbers: the harm a weapon inflicts (p. 19), journey ranks by distance (p. 111), and the Mystic Backlash table's use when a ritual fails (p. 44).
